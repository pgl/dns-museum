At the BBC and Yahoo Hack Day in June 2007, John McKerrell put his latitude and longitude into the name he asked his DNS server to look up. The server read the coordinates and sent them to Yahoo's Fire Eagle location service. A counter gave each update a fresh name so that a cached answer would not stop it reaching his server.

McKerrell set up mapme.at to host the code. By 2008, the site could log locations from DNS, email and a mobile app. [McKerrell's account of mapme.at](https://blog.johnmckerrell.com/2008/08/28/location-tracking-on-a-geo-social-network/)

After 13 years, McKerrell announced that the site would close on 9 October 2020. [Closure announcement](https://blog.mapme.at/)
