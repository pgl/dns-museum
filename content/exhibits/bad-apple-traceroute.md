A traceroute normally shows a list of hops. In this July 2026 experiment, the digits of the IPv6 addresses form a picture when the rows line up. As the addresses change, the silhouettes from the Bad Apple!! video move across the terminal.

The author reduced the video to 30 by 11 pixels at eight frames per second and modified the traceroute tool `mtr` to show one current address per hop. The picture is in the address digits themselves.
