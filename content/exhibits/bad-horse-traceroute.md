The `bad.horse` route turns reverse DNS into the lyrics of Bad Horse’s message from *Dr Horrible’s Sing-Along Blog*.

The zone also became known for `signed.bad.horse`, a certificate-chain variation on the same idea. The route is a compact example of DNS names used as a narrative surface.
