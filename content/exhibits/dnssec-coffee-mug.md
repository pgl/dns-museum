Paul Roberts dropped his Infoblox mug in June 2026 and posted photos of the pieces. You can still read part of the DNS answer for `expert.infoblox.com` on the black ceramic.

Two people who remembered the mug replied to Roberts. Hans den Otter recalled that it promoted DNSSEC, the system for signing DNS records. Chris Marrison said he had designed it 21 years earlier.
