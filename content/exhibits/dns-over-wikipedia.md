Aaron Janse's 2020 extension was for people who knew a site's name but had forgotten its address. When someone typed a name ending in `.idk`, the extension searched Wikipedia, read the official website link from the article's infobox and redirected the browser.

The lookup took place inside the browser. The project did not need a delegation for .idk in the DNS root. [Janse's launch discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22790425)
