How can a gymnastics club check who recognises its national federation? In a 2024 presentation, Werner Staub showed how an international federation could name a member federation in a TXT record under its own domain.

DNSSEC would let a reader check which domain published the statement. Staub's slides distinguish a federation making its own statement from a third party reporting the same relationship.
