Austria's amateur radio network, HAMNET, carries IP traffic over radio links. Its DNS guide gives the example of a web radio receiver at `websdr.oe7xgr.hamip.at`. An operator can use the receiver's name without knowing its IP address.

Radio links also carried the zone transfers that kept copies of the network's DNS records in sync. The Austrian amateur radio association wrote its guide around 2015 and updated the domain name to `hamip.at` in 2025.
